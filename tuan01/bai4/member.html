Membership Options

Diamond Health Club offers a variety of membership programs. Pick up a membership form at the club or e-mail Ron Chi to request one.

Individual memberships

You must be eighteen or older to qualify for an individual membership.

  • Gold: Full access to the club and access to special Gold Member areas.
  • Full: Full access to the club during all open hours.
  • Weekdays: Full access from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., Monday through Friday.
  • Evenings: Access from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. every day.
  • Early Bird: Access from 5 a.m. to 8 a.m. every day.
  • Weekends: Saturday and Sunday. Perfect for weekend warriors.
  • Seniors: Special rates for individual seniors.

Family memberships

  • With child care: For children ages 6 weeks to 12 years.
  • Without child care: Save a little if your family won't need the play room.
  • Couples: Married couples or domestic partners.
  • Seniors: Special rates for senior couples.

Temporary memberships

  • Monthly: Access for one month.
  • Weekly: One week of access.
  • Daily: A daily pass.
  • Punch cards: Available for 5, 10, and 20 visits.
  • Special events: Plan your parties and work outings at DHC.
