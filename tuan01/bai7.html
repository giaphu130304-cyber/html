Body Systems LSM400 Linear Smith Machine

Overview

Linear Smith Machines are the ultimate in weight machines and the LSM400 leads the field. Designed for both serious fitness users and beginners, the LSM400's vertical design provides all of the safety and control associated with Smith Machines. You don't even need a workout partner.

You can tailor the LSM400 to meet your specific workout needs. Start with the basic package and add a lat/row, pec dec, plate tree, dip, and accessory organizer. The LSM400 works with all standard Olympic weight plates.

Features

  • Smooth Smith Action: The bar (rated at 800 lb. capacity) slides on Teflon bushings alongside solid steel guide rods.
  • Proper Angle: The bar travels along a 7 degree path from vertical.
  • Safety: 20 lock-out points.
  • Rugged: Heavy 12 gauge steel and 7 gauge reinforcing plate.
  • Warranty: Lifetime guarantee.
